# RENKOO Build Order

0. Foundation: Docker, PostgreSQL, Redis, Prisma, NestJS, Next.js, health check.
1. Identity: register, login, JWT, refresh, logout, hashing, rate limits, organizations.
2. Client onboarding: Business Brain, website, industry, locations, services, goals.
3. Website intelligence: crawler, technical SEO, indexability, metadata, internal links, schema.
4. Search data: GSC, GA4, keywords, competitors, visibility.
5. AEO/GEO: AI query sets, visibility, citations, competitor comparison.
6. RENKOO AI Assistant: explain what is happening, why, what to do, what RENKOO can execute, approvals.
7. AI workers: SEO research, content, technical, local, AI visibility, links, analytics, reporting.
8. Execution: approvals, safe automation, audit log, rollback where possible.
9. Agency: multiple clients, health, bulk monitoring, white label, reports.
10. SaaS: plans, usage, billing, teams, API, enterprise security.

Rule: complete and verify one checkpoint before moving to the next.
